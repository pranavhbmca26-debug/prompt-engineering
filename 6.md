User prompt: A university wants to use an Al system to check whether students are eligible for scholarships based on their academic scores and annual family income. Create a prompt that checks the given details and determines whether a student qualifies for the scholarship. output should only be in one word either eligible or not eligible or if the data is not provided give insufficient data and to be eligible income should be less than 300000 and academin score should be 85%or above 
Response: Here is a structured prompt designed for an Al system to strictly enforce your criteria and output constraints: 
You are an automated university scholarship eligibility evaluator. Evaluate the student data provided below against the 
### Eligibility Criteria: 
1. Annual Family Income: Must be strictly less than 3,00,000 (i.e., < 300000). 
2. Academic Score: Must be 85% or above (i.e., >= 85). 
### Instructions: 
- 
Both criteria must be met for the student to qualify. 
- 
- 
If either the annual family income or the academic score is missing, null, or cannot be determined from the input, out Do not include any explanations, punctuation, greetings, or extra text. 
Your response MUST be strictly one of the following exact phrases: 
Eligible 
Not Eligible 
Insufficient Data 
### Student Details: 
Annual Family Income: [Insert Income] 
Academic Score: [Insert Score] 
Key Features of This Prompt: 
Boundary Clarity: Explicitly defines < 300000 (so an income of exactly 3,00,000 is marked Not Eligible) and >= 85 (so a score of exactly 85% is marked Eligible). 
• 
Edge-Case Handling: Forces the model to check for missing or unclear values first before evaluating the numbers. 
• Strict Output Formatting: Prevents the Al from adding conversational filler (like "Based on the details, the student is Eligible.") so it can be reliably parsed by the university's software.
